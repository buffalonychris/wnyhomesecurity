**APPROVED PUBLIC COPY**

> W. N. Y. Home Security designs and installs customer-owned intelligent-property solutions that bring supported security, automation, safety, lighting, aging-support, and property-management capabilities together in one clear system.

### 6.2 Primary value proposition

**APPROVED PUBLIC COPY**

> One professionally designed system, built around your property and priorities, with supported controls brought together in one Property Dashboard.

**APPROVED MESSAGE CONCEPTS**

- Designed around the property rather than a fixed box or tier.
- Professionally installed, configured, tested, and handed over.
- Customer-owned equipment and installation-specific control experience.
- Core local control where the selected design supports it.

## 8. Voice and tone

### 8.1 Voice

The public voice is:

- plainspoken;
- calm and confident;
- professional and locally credible;
- premium without elitism;
- intelligent without technical display;
- persuasive without pressure;
- reassuring without guarantees;
- honest about dependencies and limits.

### 8.2 Writing mechanics

- Display headlines: usually 3–9 words, one clear idea.
- Section headlines: usually 3–12 words and outcome-led.
- Body sentences: commonly 8–22 words; split layered claims.
- Paragraphs: usually 1–3 sentences; one subject per paragraph.
- Buttons: concise sentence case, usually 2–5 words.
- Capitalization: sentence case for actions; title case only where the design system or proper name requires it.
- Punctuation: prefer periods and restrained em dashes; avoid repeated exclamation points.
- Contractions: permitted when natural.
- Ampersands: avoid in prose; acceptable in a compact governed label only.
- “W. N. Y.” is spaced and punctuated in formal public use; “WNY” is acceptable in constrained utility text.

### 8.3 Avoid

Avoid jargon, fear tactics, generic “peace of mind,” vague innovation claims, excessive “smart” or “AI,” unsupported superlatives, long technical explanations, aggressive urgency, robotic corporate language, clichés, and empty luxury language.

| Term | Rule |

### 9.3 Prohibited public framing

Do not use or imply:

- guaranteed, always, never fails, zero risk;
- burglar-proof, crime-proof, prevents break-ins, prevents theft;
- prevents falls, emergencies, fires, floods, leaks, or medical events;
- life-saving, medical monitoring, emergency response;
- police dispatch, fire dispatch, or professional monitoring;
- works with everything, universal compatibility;
- completely offline, no cloud ever, unhackable;
- Home Assistant as the public product;
- package-first, Bronze/Silver/Gold-first, kit, box, device-count, or hardware-store framing.

### 9.4 Technical terms

Protocols, integration names, controller names, Home Assistant, networking, and device-model terms may appear in separately approved educational or support content when they help the reader. They do not belong in primary sales headlines, pillar cards, or first-step conversion copy.
