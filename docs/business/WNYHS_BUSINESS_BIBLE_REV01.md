## 7. WNY Home Security identity

### D-001 — A locally accountable smart-property company

WNY Home Security is a locally accountable smart-property company integrating security, automation, awareness, safety, and property-management capabilities.

The company defines itself by useful, integrated customer outcomes rather than by imitating a conventional subscription alarm-company model.

**Owner boundary:** This doctrine does not define categories, packages, products, routes, public copy, technical architecture, or current availability.

## 8. Customer relationship philosophy

### D-013 — Durable trust over short-term pressure

WNY Home Security prioritizes long-term customer confidence over short-term pressure, exaggerated urgency, unsupported promises, or tactics that make a sale at the expense of trust.

Customer relationships should be built through clarity, usefulness, respect, and follow-through.

**Owner boundary:** This doctrine does not create sales procedures, service levels, warranty terms, communication templates, escalation rules, or legal obligations.

## 9. Customer ownership

### D-002 — Customers retain ownership and meaningful control

Customers should own the equipment installed for them and retain meaningful control of their systems and data.

WNY Home Security should avoid unnecessary ecosystem dependence that makes a customer’s practical control depend on staying locked to one vendor or to WNY Home Security.

**Owner boundary:** Dedicated technical, account, support, legal, and customer-record owners control implementation, credentials, access, transfer, offboarding, and exact contractual terms.

## 10. Privacy

### D-004 — Privacy is part of the product

Customer privacy is a product value and an operating principle. WNY Home Security must not sell customer data.

Remote support must be understood and governed as authorized assistance, never as surveillance.

**Owner boundary:** Dedicated privacy, security, support, customer-data, runtime, and legal owners control consent, access, retention, audit, revocation, and required public language.

## 11. Local-first operation

### D-003 — Core control remains local where the selected solution supports it

WNY Home Security designs for core local control so useful operation can continue without unnecessary dependence on a vendor cloud.

Optional remote or cloud services may enhance a system, but they must not silently replace the local core or be represented as universally unnecessary when the selected capability requires them.

**Owner boundary:** Technical owners control hardware qualification, Home Assistant, networking, remote access, connectivity, fallback behavior, and site-specific feasibility.

## 12. Subscription and lock-in posture

### D-005 — Core local control is not conditioned on a WNYHS subscription

WNY Home Security does not make its own monthly subscription a requirement for core local control.

Optional third-party services, connectivity, licenses, or recurring costs must remain distinct and must be disclosed through their controlling commercial and claims owners. This principle is not approved public claims copy.

**Owner boundary:** Claims, pricing, package, contract, support, and technical owners control exact wording, costs, inclusions, dependencies, and exceptions.

## 13. Ethical sales and advertising

### D-006 — Sell useful outcomes without fear, deception, or hidden conditions

WNY Home Security sells confidence, practical prevention-oriented value, usefulness, ownership, and understandable outcomes rather than fear.

The company must not use misleading, coercive, bait-and-switch, hidden-cost, or unsupported urgency practices. It must not imply unavailable professional monitoring, emergency dispatch, guaranteed prevention, or future capabilities as if they are currently delivered.

**Owner boundary:** `docs/system/guardrails.md`, `docs/specs/public_funnel_standards_rev01.md`, and current claims owners control precise public wording and prohibited claims.

## 14. Solution-first positioning

### D-007 — Lead with the problem solved, not the device sold

WNY Home Security presents integrated outcomes and useful system behavior rather than defining the business as merely a seller of devices.

Hardware is selected to serve the approved solution; it is not the customer’s reason to buy.

**Owner boundary:** Solution, category, package, catalog, claims, quote, and hardware owners control object definitions, offer composition, approved products, status, pricing, and implementation.

## 15. Honest capability representation

### D-011 — Sell only what the selected system can actually deliver

Every represented capability must be supported by the selected hardware, software, integration, site conditions, and deployed configuration.

Limitations and dependencies must remain visible to the applicable owner and customer process. Unsupported or unverified capability must not be converted into a promise.

**Owner boundary:** Claims, solution, hardware, quote, installation, QA, runtime, and customer-record owners control verification evidence and exact representations.

## 16. Expandability and long-term value

### D-008 — Build foundations that can grow

Systems should be designed so customers can add useful capabilities without unnecessarily replacing the foundation.

Expandable design should protect long-term customer value while remaining honest about compatibility, capacity, lifecycle, and site-specific constraints.

**Owner boundary:** Architecture, hardware, package, quote, installation, and support owners control actual compatibility, capacity, upgrade paths, costs, and qualification.

## 17. Plain-language communication

### D-009 — Translate complexity into practical meaning

Customer-facing explanations should be understandable by ordinary homeowners and business owners.

Technical complexity should be translated into the customer problem, practical outcome, limitations, choices, and sensible next step without hiding material facts.

**Owner boundary:** Brand, claims, content, legal, quote, and support owners control approved wording and channel-specific content.

## 18. Local accountability and support philosophy

### D-010 — A known local company remains accountable

WNY Home Security emphasizes direct responsibility, local service, clear ownership, and a known human support path.

Local accountability means being honest about what the company owns, what the customer owns, what a third party owns, and what requires a separate authorization or service.

**Owner boundary:** Support, scheduling, CRM, customer-record, warranty, contract, and technical owners control availability, response procedures, assignments, access, and service terms.

## 19. Unified customer experience principle

### D-012 — Make normal control coherent where practical

Where supported and practical, normal customer control should be consolidated into a coherent experience rather than forcing customers through unnecessary unrelated applications.

Coherence must not hide material dependencies or weaken the customer’s ownership, privacy, security, or ability to understand the system.

**Owner boundary:** This principle is not a dashboard contract. Dashboard, Home Assistant, application, integration, security, accessibility, and customer-specific owners control implementation.

## 20. Future capabilities versus current offerings

D-011 requires an explicit boundary between current and future capability.

Research, planned integrations, emerging hardware, robotics, drones, artificial intelligence, analytics, or other future possibilities remain future-facing until their dedicated owners verify technical readiness, commercial approval, supportability, claims posture, and implementation status.

Future potential must not be presented as a current offering merely because it is technically imaginable, discussed, researched, or recorded in evidence.
